# Code of Conduct

RiffPlayer is a friendly, volunteer-run project. Everyone is welcome, whatever their background or experience level.

## Expected behaviour

- Be respectful and patient, especially with people new to self-hosting or to open source.
- Give feedback on the work, not the person. Assume good intent.
- Keep discussions on topic and constructive.

## Not acceptable

- Harassment, insults, discrimination or personal attacks.
- Publishing other people's private information.
- Spam and advertising unrelated to the project.

## Enforcement

The maintainer may edit, hide or delete comments, issues and pull requests, and may lock threads or block people who don't follow these rules. To report a problem, mention the maintainer in the thread or use GitHub's **Report content** option; for something sensitive, use the private channel described in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md) and mark it as a conduct report.

This applies to all project spaces: the issue tracker, pull requests, discussions and any official social accounts.
